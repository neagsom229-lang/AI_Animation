# video-shotcraft Skill

## Overview
Provides professional Remotion shot recipes, camera transitions, and multi-shot sequencing guidelines.

## Guidelines
- Use standard shot durations (Wide establishing: 90 frames, Close-up dialogue: 60 frames, Action/Tracking: 120 frames).
- Apply smooth easing curves (`Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`) on camera cuts and transitions.
- Maintain 30 fps or 60 fps frame-deterministic contracts.
